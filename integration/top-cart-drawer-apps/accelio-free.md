# ⭐ Accelio (Free)

### Why BOGOS loves Accelio

Want a cart that looks great and makes your BOGOS offers shine? Meet <a href="https://apps.shopify.com/accelio-cart-drawer?utm_source=BOGOS&amp;utm_medium=Help_Doc&amp;utm_campaign=Integration_Accelio" target="_blank">Accelio</a>, a free cart drawer app and our top-recommended cart drawer partner.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (547).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Why we love it:

* **Free.** You don't have to pay anything for Accelio.
* **Smooth and always in sync.** It works the most smoothly with any BOGOS offer and keeps up with every BOGOS update.
* **Beautiful and customizable.** A well-designed slide cart drawer you can change to match your store.
* **Instant updates.** When BOGOS adds a gift, or a shopper adds or removes one in the gift slider, the drawer updates right away. No page reload.
* **Easy to extend.** Drag and drop BOGOS blocks, like the gift slider, today's offer widget, or progress bar, right into your cart.

{% hint style="success" %}
Want to see it in action? View the BOGOS x Accelio demo in [our demo store](https://demo-store-by-bogos.myshopify.com/?utm_source=BOGOS\&utm_medium=Help_docs\&utm_campaign=Integration_Accelio).
{% endhint %}

### How to integrate BOGOS with Accelio

**Step 1: Set up your BOGOS offers.** Create the offers you want to run, such as a free gift or a bundle.

**Step 2: Install** <a href="https://apps.shopify.com/accelio-cart-drawer?utm_source=BOGOS&amp;utm_medium=Help_Doc&amp;utm_campaign=Integration_Accelio" target="_blank">Accelio Slide Cart Drawer</a> from the Shopify App Store & customize your cart.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (546).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="success" %}
Your BOGOS offers will automatically work smoothly in the Accelio drawer from now and then. You do not need to do anything else.
{% endhint %}

**Step 3 (Optional):** Click Add block > Choose Apps> Then drag and drop BOGOS blocks into your cart, such as the gift slider, today's offer widget, or progress bar.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (544).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
