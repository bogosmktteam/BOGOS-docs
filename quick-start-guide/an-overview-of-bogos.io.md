# An overview of BOGOS.io

{% embed url="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iXhltERkudE" %}

Increase AOV and drive more sales with BOGOS through powerful offers: BOGO, Buy x get y, gift with purchase, Bundle builder, Mix & match, Upsell, and Discounts.

With the help of BOGOS.io, customers will be happy to discover a broad choice of excellent offers with their purchase.&#x20;

We offer a highly skilled technical support team through live chat and email. Our team is responsive 24/24 during weekdays from Monday to Friday.

## Available offer types

### 1. Gift offers

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (116).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Choose a gift offer template</p></figcaption></figure>

**Uses cases:**&#x20;

* Buy one get one
* Buy X get Y free
* Spend amount to get a gift(s)
* Free sample with purchase
* Spend more get more

**Key features:**&#x20;

* Automatically add gifts to customers’ carts
* Display in the gift slider
* Target the right audience with the main conditions and sub-conditions
* Manage multiple offers with priority
* Work with POS, Mobile App, Headless/Hydrogen

**Main conditions:**&#x20;

* Cart value condition
* Cart quality condition
* Specific product condition
* Cart value multiplier condition
* Pack of products condition&#x20;

**Sub-conditions:**&#x20;

* Special link address
* Customers’ order history
* Customer tags
* Customer location
* Subscription products
* Sales channels

### 2. Bundle offer

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (117).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Choose a bundle type</p></figcaption></figure>

**Types of Bundles:**

* Classic bundle: A fixed set of products sold together
* Mix and match: Allows customers to create their own bundle from a selection of items
* Bundle page: A dedicated landing page allows customers to customize their bundles

**Use cases:**

* Buy a bundle of Product A and Product B to get a specific amount off
* Mix and match favorite items from a list to get a lower price
* A single bundle page provides multiple offers

**Key features:**

* Customizable widget for bundle offers
* Flexibly combine different products or variants
* Design and customize their own bundle on a single page

### 3. Upsell offer

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (118).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Choose an upsell offer type</p></figcaption></figure>

**Types of Upsell:**&#x20;

* Checkout upsell: An effective way to capture last-minute additions during the payment process
* Frequently bought together: Upsell your customers with relevant products&#x20;
* Thank you page upsell: This taps into shoppers at their most receptive moment after successfully purchasing the main products

**Use cases:**

* Add-on product for $X when cart includes Product Y
* Display on touchpoint pages such as: Product page, Checkout page, and Thank you page&#x20;

**Key features:**

* Shown as perfectly timed popups&#x20;
* Trigger upsells based on customers’ order history, products, or cart data
* Customize upsell widgets and popups to align with your brand

### 4. Discount offer

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (119).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Choose a discount type</p></figcaption></figure>

**Use cases:**

* Volume discounts: Buy 3, get 10% off; Buy 5, get 20% off
* Quantity breaks for single or multiple products
* Tiered pricing models

**Key features:**

* Flexible discount levels based on quantity or spend
* Manual or automatic discount code application
* Target specific SKUs or product categories
* Real-time inventory sync across all channels

### 5. All offer dashboard

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (47).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

This All Offers dashboard is the place for you to manage all the offers created with the BOGOS app. Offers are displayed in a grid format with basic information to help you easily track offer status:

* **Title**: Internal offer name for your management (customers won't see this except for gift offer and classic bundle)
* **Offer type**: Main promotion types of BOGOS
* **Start date**: Time when the offer begins
* **Status**: There are 4 offer status types&#x20;

▶ Active: Currently running offers

▶ Deactivated: Turned off offers

▶ Scheduled: Offers that are set to start at a future date

▶ Expired: Offers that have passed their end date

▶ Locked: Offers from paid plans that get locked when customers downgrade to the free plan

▶ Incomplete: Offers imported from CSV files that haven't been fully set up with the required conditions to publish <br>

* **On/off**: Switch button that helps merchants easily change between active and deactivated status for offers
* **Actions**: This allows you to duplicate or delete any offer

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (48).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="success" %}
BOGOS also groups offers by status, making it easy to find the specific offers you need to adjust.
{% endhint %}

**Export and Import**

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (329).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Export**&#x20;

This feature lets you export offer data from your current store to import into another store or use later.<br>

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (330).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

After choosing this option, **a CSV file** of your store’s offer data will be sent to the email address you enter in the box below.<br>

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (331).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

There are **6 options** for you to select before exporting: all offers, selected offers, gift offer only, bundle offer only, upsell offer only, and discount offer only.

**Import**

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (332).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

This feature lets you upload offer data from a CSV file directly into your store.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (333).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Once you've selected a CSV file from your computer, choose one (or both) of the following options:

* **Select products for offers with matching SKU:** Automatically insert products in your store that share the same SKU as those in the imported offers.
* **Select collection for offers with matching handle:** Automatically insert collections in your store that share the same handle as those in the imported offers.

{% hint style="info" %}
Make sure the SKUs and handles in your online store match those in the imported file so the mapping process works properly.
{% endhint %}

**Other things to bear in mind:**

* The imported offer will work if any product SKUs match. If none match, the offer will be marked as incomplete.
* During import, only the offer conditions and products are mapped; all the offer statuses will be converted to deactivated.
