# Feature Vote

Using Feature Vote includes 3 parts:

1\. Suggest a feature.

2\. Feature board.

3\. My suggestion.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (459).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### 1. Suggest a Feature

You can send a new suggestion to the BOGOS team from either tab in Feature Vote.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (460).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

To submit a suggestion, please follow these steps:

1\. Click **+Suggest a feature** at the top-right corner. You can also click **Send a suggestion** from the empty state of the **My suggestion** tab.

2\. Fill in these fields:

* **Your suggestion:** The feature you want to suggest (required, up to 500 characters).
* **Tell us more:** Extra details about what you are trying to do or how this feature would help your store (optional, up to 5000 characters).

3\. Click **Submit suggestion**.

Your suggestion is now sent to the BOGOS team and appears under **My suggestion** > **Under review**.

#### 2. Feature Board&#x20;

The Feature board shows all suggestions currently open for voting, so you can support the ideas that matter most to your store.

**2.1. Highlight**

Features hand-picked by BOGOS, shown at the top of the board. Click a card to open the **Feature detail** popup.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (461).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**2.2. Feature board**

The full list of suggestions. To find features faster, you can:

* Filter by status: **All**, **In progress**, or **Released**.
* Sort by: **Most votes** (default) or **Newest first**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (462).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

For Released features, a **Go to this feature** link appears in the Feature detail popup so you can open the feature directly in the BOGOS app.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (463).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**2.3. Vote for a feature**

To vote, click the up-arrow button (e.g., 0 voted) on a Highlight card or a Feature board row. The vote count goes up by 1, and the button turns dark to show your vote.

To remove your vote, click the same button again.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (464).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**2.4. View feature details**

To see the full details of a feature, click the ⓘ icon. The **Feature detail** popup shows the feature title, description, and current status.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (465).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### 3. My Suggestion Tab

The My suggestion tab shows all suggestions you have submitted, grouped by review status.

**3.1. Under review**

Suggestions the BOGOS team is currently reviewing. Each card shows your suggestion title and the status "Being reviewed by our team".

At this stage, you can:

* **Edit your suggestion:** Click the pencil icon on the card > Open the Edit your suggestion popup > Update the fields > Click Save changes.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (471).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* **Delete your suggestion:** Click the ⓘ icon on the card > Open the Suggestion detail popup > Click Delete suggestion > Click Delete on the Remove this suggestion? Dialog.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (467).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="warning" %}
Edit and Delete are only available while a suggestion is under review.
{% endhint %}

**3.2. Reviewed**

Suggestions the BOGOS team has already reviewed. Each card shows the suggestion title, a status badge, and an ⓘ icon to open the **Suggestion detail** popup.

Status badges include Approved / Not approved / In progress / Released. For Released features, a **Go to this feature** link also appears on the card so you can open the feature directly in the BOGOS app.

Click the ⓘ icon to open the Suggestion detail popup, which shows:

* **Your suggestion:** Your original submission.
* **Tell us more:** The extra details you added.
* **Status:** The current review status of your suggestion.
* **Suggestion:** The published feature on the Feature board created from your suggestion, along with its current vote count (available for Approved, In progress, and Released suggestions).

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (469).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
