# What is BOGOS App?

{% embed url="https://youtu.be/iXhltERkudE?si=3igBuOf1tnKF5-8F" %}

## Overview of BOGOS

[BOGOS: Free Gift Bundle Upsell](https://apps.shopify.com/freegifts?utm_source=HelpDoc\&utm_medium=IntrotoBOGOS) is a **one-stop promotion app** for Shopify merchants to create **essential to advanced sales promotions.**

Over the past 11+ years, BOGOS has helped **68,000+ brands worldwide** run high-converting offers, generate more orders, while still elevating the shopping experience.

## Available Offer Types

BOGOS supports multiple offer types to easily encourage bigger purchases and increase AOV:

* [**Gift offers**](../detailed-guide/gift-offer/) to automatically add gifts to customer carts, or let them choose one from a list, based on cart value, quantity, or specific products purchased.
* [**Bundle offers** ](../detailed-guide/bundle-offer/)to create fixed product bundles or allow customers to mix and match, or even build their own on a separate page.
* [**Upsell offers**](../detailed-guide/upsell-offer/) to display product recommendations at checkout, on product pages, and on thank you pages.
* [**Discount offers**](../detailed-guide/discount-offer/) to reward customers for purchasing in larger quantities.

For more details, please visit [BOGOS Feature Overview](bogos-feature-overview.md).

## Pricing Models

BOGOS offers 2 plans for you to choose from:

* **Free plan:** Get 30 lifetime orders to explore BOGOS' key features and see how it works for your store before committing.
* **Paid plan:** Unlock unlimited orders and full advanced features with a 7-day free trial. Each plan comes with a monthly free order limit, and usage charges apply only when you exceed that limit, capped at a maximum amount based on your Shopify plan.

For detailed pricing plans, visit the \[[BOGOS Pricing page](https://bogos.io/pricing/)].
