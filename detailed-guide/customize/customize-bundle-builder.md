# Customize Bundle builder

## Customize Bundle Builder

This guide explains how to customize the Bundle Builder, helping you tailor the bundle page to match your store's branding and create a smooth shopping experience for your customers.

Customizing the Bundle Builder with BOGOS includes the 4 steps:

1. In BOGOS Admin, open **Customize** > **Bundle** > **Bundle builder**.
2. Customize the **Bundle product field**.
3. Customize the **Bundle summary field**.
4. Click Save.

### 1. Customize Bundle Product Field

The Bundle product field is where customers browse products and add them to their bundle. This tab lets you customize the layout, content, and styling of that section.

#### a) Content

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/unknown (434).png>)

To set it up, fill in these fields:

* Button text
* When product is out of stock
* When reach maximum quantity
* Number of products per load: Set how many products are displayed per load, with separate settings for Desktop and Mobile:
  * Products per row&#x20;
  * Number of rows
  * Products per load
* Load button text: The label on the button customers click to load more products.

#### b) See more modal

The See more modal appears when customers click on a product card, showing product images, description, and variants, so customers can view details without leaving the bundle page.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/unknown (435).png>)

To set it up, fill in these fields:

* Thumbnail gallery position: Choose where the thumbnail gallery appears in the modal:
* Below main image
* Left of main image

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/unknown (436).png>)

* Link to product page label: Enable to show a clickable link in the modal that takes customers to the full product page. When enabled, enter the label text (e.g., "See product's details").
* Select icon: Choose an icon from the available options, or upload your own to match your brand.

{% hint style="warning" %}
- The modal displays up to 10 product images. If a product has more than 10, only the first 10 will be shown.
- For bundle offers already published before this update, only 1 image will be displayed until the offer is published again.
- If a product's images are updated on Shopify, the modal won't auto-sync. Please re-publish the offer to update the latest images.
{% endhint %}

#### c) Color

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/unknown (437).png>)

BOGOS allows you to customize the colors of all elements with any of the 4 methods below:

* Choose a ready-made color set from the dropdown.
* Manually adjust each color field for all the available colors.
* Use AI Theme Detector to auto-generate a full widget color palette based on your store's branding. For more details, visit our \[[AI Theme Detector](https://docs.bogos.io/detailed-guide/customize/ai-theme-detector)] guide.
* Contact the BOGOS support team to get help with color customization.

Click Save when done.

**Step:** Customize the colors of the step selector at the top of the bundle page, including:

* Step background
* Step text
* Step underline (under the active step)

**Page text:** Customize the colors of the text elements on the bundle page, including:

* Page heading
* Page sub-heading
* Step heading (e.g., "Add a facial cleanser")
* Step description

**Product list:** Customize the colors of the text elements on each product card, including:

* Product name
* Variant name
* Discount price
* Original price (strikethrough)

**Add button:** Customize the colors of the "Add to bundle" button on each product card, including:

* Button color (background)
* Button text

### 2. Customize Bundle Summary Field

The Bundle Summary field lets customers review their selected items, track progress toward discount tiers, and add the bundle to their cart. This tab lets you customize its content and styling.

#### a) Content

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/unknown (438).png>)

To set it up, fill in these fields:

* Title
* Show description
* Total price text
* Add to cart button text
* Tier style: Choose how discount tiers are displayed:
  * Label: Shows the tier as text (e.g., "Buy 2 items get 10% OFF").
  * Progress: Shows a visual progress bar as customers get closer to unlocking the next tier.

#### b) Step

Customize the step section that shows the required items for each step of the bundle.

* Step title
* Required product text
* Blank tex
* Select icon: Choose an icon from the available options, or upload your own to match your brand.

#### c) Color

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/unknown (439).png>)

BOGOS allows you to customize the colors of all elements with any of the 4 methods below:

* Choose a ready-made color set from the dropdown.
* Manually adjust each color field for all the available colors.
* Use AI Theme Detector to auto-generate a full widget color palette based on your store's branding. For more details, visit our \[[AI Theme Detector](https://docs.bogos.io/detailed-guide/customize/ai-theme-detector)] guide.
* Contact the BOGOS support team to get help with color customization.

Click Save when done.

**Bundle summary:** Customize the colors of the bundle summary section, including:

* Background
* Title
* Description
* Required text (the "item(s) required" text)

**Add to cart button:** Customize the colors of the "Add bundle to cart" button, including:

* Button color (background)
* Button text

**Add-on:** Add-on customization applies when your bundle offer includes free gifts or shipping discounts.

* Free gift discount, including:
  * Free product text (the label shown next to the free gift product name)
  * Free price text (the text replacing the price of the free gift, e.g., "FREE")
* Shipping discount, including:
  * Shipping icon: Choose an icon from the available options, or upload your own to match your brand.
  * Banner color (background)
  * Banner text

### 3. Adjust Widget Position on Your Store

By default, the Bundle builder appears as a separate page once the offer is published.

However, you can place the Bundle builder block on any page of your store (except the Checkout page). To do this:

1. Go to Shopify admin > Online Store.
2. Open the theme editor.
3. Navigate to the page where you want to add the widget.
4. Click **Add section** > **Apps** > select **BOGOS: Bundle Builder block**.
5. In the block settings, enter the **Offer ID** of the Bundle builder offer you want to display.

For more details, visit our \[[Adjust BOGOS Blocks](https://docs.bogos.io/quick-start-guide/adjust-bogos-blocks)] guide.
