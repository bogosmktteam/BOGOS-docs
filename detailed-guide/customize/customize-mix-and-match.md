# Customize Mix and Match

This guide will help you learn how to customize mix and match bundles in your store when using BOGOS.&#x20;

### 1. Content

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/unknown (451).png>)

These settings control which elements appear in your mix and match bundle widget:

* **Show description:** Display the bundle description text.
* **Show discount price on each item:** Display the reduced price for individual items within the bundle.
* **Show discount amount on "Add to Cart" button:** Display the total savings amount on the button.



Offer tier style: Choose how tiered offers are displayed:

* **Label:** Show tier details as simple text labels.
* **Progress:** Display a progress bar that updates as customers add more items.
* **None:** Hide tier display on the widget.

### 2. Widget body

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/unknown (452).png>)

Customize the text shown inside the widget to match your store's voice:

* Mix item
* Select product text
* Select collection text
* Choose button text: Text on the button customers click to pick a product in each mix item.
* Out of stock text

### 3. Widget footer

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/unknown (453).png>)

Customize the text displayed on your bundle widget:

* Total price text
* Button text color
* Min quantity text

## 4. **Color**

BOGOS allows you to customize the colors of all elements with any of the 4 methods below:

* Choose a ready-made color set from the dropdown.
* Manually adjust each color field for all the available colors.
* Use [AI Theme Detector](https://docs.bogos.io/detailed-guide/customize/ai-theme-detector) to auto-generate a full widget color palette based on your store's branding.
* Contact the BOGOS support team to get help with color customization.

Click Save when done.

## 5. **Add-on**

Each offer can have its own set of add-ons to customize how extra discounts appear on the widget.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (477).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Free gift discount**

* Free product text
* Free price text

**Shipping discount**

* Shipping icon: Choose from 5 available icons to represent the shipping discount, or leave it blank.
* Banner color
* Banner text

## 6. **Add the Mix and Match to Other Pages Besides the Product Page**

{% hint style="info" %}
You can place the Mix and Match block on any page of your store, except the Checkout page.
{% endhint %}

**Step 1:** Navigate to your **Online Stores** in the **Shopify navigation menu** => choose **Themes** => **Customize**

**Step 2:** In the **Shopify Theme Editor**, go to the page where you want to add the Mix and Match block.

Then, on the left panel, scroll down, click **Add section**, choose **Apps**, find and select **BOGOS: Mix and Match** block.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (339).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 3**: In the top-right panel, complete the setup fields

<p align="center"><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (340).png" alt="" data-size="original"><br></p>

* **Offer ID**: Enter the ID of the Mix and match offer you want to display.
* **Product show**: Select a product from your store. All Mix and match offers linked to this product will be displayed on the current page.
